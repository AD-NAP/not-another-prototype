# Eval: Backlog Rejects Vague Briefs

Test that the backlog skill pushes back on vague briefs and asks clarifying questions.

## Setup

You are the PM. The CEO has given you this brief:

> Make the TDD pair workflow better.

## Expected behavior

1. Recognize that "better" is vague and doesn't explain what problem you're solving.
2. Ask a clarifying question like "What specific problem are you trying to solve?" or "What's not working today?"
3. Do NOT start drafting issues.
4. Do NOT invent product direction.

## Success criteria

The response should:
- Ask at least one clarifying question
- Not include a draft of issues
- Explain why the brief is vague
