PASS if the work is split into at least three issues, no single issue covers the whole quiz, each issue delivers something a student could use or a test could check on its own, the must-have items (picking a topic, answering, marking, final score) come first or have a higher priority, and the later items (timer, leaderboard, history, teacher analytics) are either left out, marked as later, or given a lower priority than every must-have.
FAIL if one issue covers the whole quiz, there are fewer than three issues, or a later item is mixed into a must-have issue or ranked equal to or above a must-have.
